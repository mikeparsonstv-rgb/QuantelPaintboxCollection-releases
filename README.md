# Quantel Paintbox Collection — releases

The Paintbox Collection (Digital Fine Art, Digital Paint Box, Paintbox V-Series, Harry, HAL, Henry) — six Quantel machines chosen from one loading screen, the picture going to the screen through Metal.

Two editions, two update feeds. Pick one:

| Edition | For | Download |
|---|---|---|
| **Paintbox Collection** | Apple silicon Macs on **macOS 26 or later** | `PaintboxCollection-<version>.zip` |
| **Paintbox Collection (Universal)** | Apple silicon **and Intel** Macs on **macOS 15 Sequoia or later** | `PaintboxCollection-Universal-<version>.zip` |

Download the newest zip of your edition from Releases, unzip it and move Paintbox Collection to Applications. Installed copies update themselves within their edition (Paintbox Collection › Check for Updates…). The two editions are the same software; the Universal one simply runs on more Macs.
